Palette: existing white surface #ffffff, light ground #f6f7f7, ink #1d2926, muted #52605b, green #17634f and alert #b43b35. Dark mode uses existing kit tokens. Keep self-hosted Geist throughout, 44–58px hero, 30–36px section headings, 15–16px body, comfortable line height. No new visual identity or component library.

Layout: left-aligned introduction faces a read-only map sequence. Underneath: Power / Wi-Fi / Conditions. Follow with first-responder review, local recording and power conservation, actual hardware, then team/event/services and GitHub. One plain simulation caption belongs to the preview. Event attribution belongs in About, not above/below the hero. Existing private repository link must be labeled as access-required; do not change repository visibility.

Critique: avoid repeating the earlier pile of pills, frame captions and footer disclaimers. Use spacing, section hierarchy and written status instead. Keep safety-relevant unknown/stale labels but remove their button-like styling. No simulated condition is a claim about live homes. Battery endurance remains a design goal under test, and first-responder support is an intended workflow, not an established dispatch integration.

Interaction: real Argus dropdowns for theme/motion; a plain Settings control at the upper right; sidebar width and labels move together. The homepage sequence loops like a short animation, has Pause, respects reduced motion and pauses offscreen. The full operator workspace remains interactive.
