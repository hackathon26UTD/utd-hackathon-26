# Stale and unknown rules

- Missing data is unknown. It is never normal and never safe.
- A box that has not reported recently is offline. Offline alone is never critical and never normal. A quiet box tells you nothing about the home.
- A stale critical reading stays critical and must be described as "last reported N minutes ago". Only a newer fresh reading replaces it. Time passing never clears it.
- A stale watch or elevated reading makes the box unknown with raised priority, not normal.
- A reading outside its valid range is discarded, not clamped.
- Wi-Fi association is not internet access. A relayed or replayed reading keeps its original observation time; receipt time is separate.
- Flame and sound inputs are proxies, not certified detectors. Water raw counts are not depth. Motion does not prove a home is empty.
- External feeds (weather, flood zones, shelters, fire detections, air quality, grid demand) are cached regional context. They never change a box status and never describe a particular home. Forecast rain is not observed rain. Grid demand is not a household outage. A feed that is missing, empty or needs a key was not consulted.
